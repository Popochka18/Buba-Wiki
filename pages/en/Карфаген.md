## Army

The army of Carthage is built from units of fixed size, the largest of which is the Hel.

A Hel consists of two Legions of 10,000 soldiers each. At its peak, the army of Carthage was made up of one hundred Hels.

## History

Originally the empire's capital was [[Тир|Tyre]], but it lay too far from the empire's eastern domains and made the sea routes difficult, so it was decided to move the capital to [[Карфаген(Город)|Carthage]].

### The Beginning of the Conquests

The empire's first victim was the city of [[Мур|Mur]], on the western coast of [[Арва|Arva]]; all men over the age of sixteen were executed, and the women and children sold into slavery.

A group of Carthaginians fled the cities and founded the city of [[Хаифа|Haifa]], calling themselves the [[Ахентцы|Ahentians]].

The emperor would not tolerate such an insult and destroyed the city, after which the [[Ахентцы|Ahentians]] were forced to wander.

### Alexander's Conquests

[[Александр Великий|Alexander the Great]] crushed the army of Carthage at [[Триполи|Tripoli]], after which he installed his own commander, [[Борис Македонский|Boris]], in power in Carthage.

After [[Александр Великий|Alexander]]'s death, [[Борис Македонский|Boris]] was overthrown by the [[Баркиды|Barcids]]. The [[Баркиды|Barcids]] ruled right up until the fall of the empire.

### The Last Tilian–Carthaginian War

The second book describes the events of the last Tilian–Carthaginian War, in which Carthage utterly crushed the army of [[Тилия|Tilia]] and laid the foundation for its absolute hegemony in the old world.

## Book 1

### Chapter 1: Three Hunters

From the very first chapter we are shown how Carthage exploits the entire population of its country.

[[Масиаф|Masyaf]] is one of the largest cities of the empire, yet most of its population are [[Кубры|Kubrs]] — perhaps because of this, the rules in [[Масиаф|Masyaf]] were far gentler than in the rest of the empire.

### Chapter 3: The King of the Bedouins

The third chapter describes Carthage's attitude toward nomadic tribes and barbarian tribes.

Carthage enslaves all who can be called barbarians.

### Chapter 4: To Where the Sun Is

The chapter begins with a Carthaginian overseer waking [[Владимир|Vladimir]].

Vladimir is aboard a slave-transport ship.
