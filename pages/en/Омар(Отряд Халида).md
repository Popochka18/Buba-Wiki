## Personality

For all his imposing build and thunderous voice, Omar hides a deep vulnerability: rumour has it that he wept at night when he thought no one could hear. Before the battle, he is the first to ask Amir the question that troubles so many in the squad — "why do you fight, if it brings you pain?" — asking it in a quiet, almost whispering voice that looks especially strange coming from a man of his stature.

On the evening before the battle, he talks with [[Али(Отряд Халида)|Ali]], [[Амир аль-Фарис|Amir]] and [[Фарид|Farid]] while a pot of soup simmers.

As they await the attack, Omar stays calmer than the rest: while [[Фарид|Farid]] prays and [[Касим|Qasim]] sharpens an already keen knife, Omar simply sits with his eyes closed, breathing deep and even — whether asleep or meditating, no one can tell. When the time comes, he opens his eyes with the clear, calm gaze of a man who has accepted his fate.

## Death

In the battle that follows, Omar — despite his size — moves "surprisingly quietly." He falls under the blows of three Carthaginian soldiers' swords at once, who hack at him until he stops moving. Amir later remembers his death as one of those losses that he and Farid — the only two survivors out of forty-three — carry with them as the memory of their fallen comrades.
