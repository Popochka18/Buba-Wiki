Old Babylon (not to be confused with [[Вавилон Великий|Babylon the Great]] — a state that existed before the collapse, at the end of the first era).

According to legend, the king of Babylon ordered a search for the Vessel of Weeping, in which Chernobog was imprisoned.

The ruins of the tower that once stood in Babylon were found in the eastern part of Aramea, at the foot of the White Mountains, which confirms that the empire existed.
