## History

Little is known about the existence of the White Hand before the events of the second book. The White Hand works closely with pirates and Carthage, plundering lands and enslaving the barbarian tribes of [[Арва|Arva]], chiefly the [[Берберы|Berbers]] and the [[Бедуины|Bedouins]].
They practice cannibalism, though they do not eat white-skinned people.

## Book Two

### Chapter 3: The House of al-Fahri

The tribe is first mentioned in a dialogue between [[Мутассим аль-Фахри|Mutassim]] and [[Юсуф аль-Фахри|Yusuf]], when [[Мутассим аль-Фахри|Mutassim]] asks about taking the longer route.

They first appear in that same chapter as dark figures on the crest of the hill nearest the caravan.

![Black_barbarian_warriors_on_dune_202606132246.jpeg](Black_barbarian_warriors_on_dune_202606132246.jpeg)

They win the fight, and Mutassim is knocked out.

### Chapter 30: How the Death Sentence Was Written

A Carthaginian messenger arrives before the tribe's chieftain with an offer to lay siege to [[Сирт|Syrtis]]. The chieftain agrees.

![Кадр_из_фильма_тёмного_фентези_202606132318.jpeg](Кадр_из_фильма_тёмного_фентези_202606132318.jpeg)

### Chapter 34: The Last Day of the City of Syrtis

A warrior of the White Hand kills Seb with a blow to the back. Aaron rushes in to avenge him, but a shot from a Carthaginian trebuchet strikes the wall.

Later, Mutassim examines the warrior's corpse.
