## History

Little is known about the Berbers. The Berbers are a group of elven tribes living in the region of [[Мур|Mur]].

In most chronicles they are mentioned as elves who fled [[Мур|Mur]] before its siege by the Carthaginian army.

The people are fairly numerous. They are the only elven people who profess the [[Вера Солнца|Faith of the Sun]], and who practice polygamy and patriarchy in their social life.
