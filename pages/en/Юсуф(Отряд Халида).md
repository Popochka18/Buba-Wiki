## Personality

Yusuf is the voice of sober calculation in the squad. When Khalid first voices the plan to attack Hannibal Barca's army, it is Yusuf who first asks how many fighters the enemy has, and dryly sums it up: "Two hundred against forty-three — not the best of odds," without fear, simply as a statement of fact. Later it is also he who notes that not everyone in the squad will agree to march to certain death — a remark to which Khalid replies that no one is stopping the unwilling from leaving.

This level-headedness is paired in Yusuf with a readiness to see things through to the end: despite his sober assessment of the odds, he stays and takes on one of the key roles in the plan.

## Role in the Ambush

Yusuf commands a group of the squad's ten strongest fighters. They take up a position at the northern entrance of the gorge, where stones on ropes have been prepared in advance — at the signal, the rockfall is meant to cut the Carthaginian army's vanguard off from the main column. When the signal is given, it is Yusuf's men who bring down tons of rock with a roar "that seemed as if it could wake the dead."

## Death

Yusuf dies in the battle that follows — a spear runs through his chest. Amir notices an expression of surprise on his face, "as if he could not believe he had been killed by those he once trained" — the bitter irony in the fate of a man who once served in the Carthaginian army and now dies at the hands of its soldiers.
