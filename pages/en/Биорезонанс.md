Bioresonance is the ability to change the shape of a soul and the perception of reality through the mind of living beings. In truth, the state of objects does not change — only the brain's perception of their position does.

Susceptibility to bioresonance — that is, the ability to wield it — is passed down by inheritance among humans. In humans, bioresonance is inherited by the seventh-born son; a human woman can neither inherit nor possess bioresonance.

[[Эльфы|Elves]] (except forest and dark elves) inherit bioresonance provided that one of their parents is susceptible to it. All [[Тёмные эльфы|dark elves]] wielded bioresonance until the [[Последнее проклятие|Last Curse]], after which they almost completely died out and were believed wholly extinct until the events of the game [[Avroria: Fetus]]. It is unknown whether [[Тёмные эльфы|dark elves]] wielded bioresonance in the 2nd and 3rd eras. [[Лесные эльфы|Forest elves]] were never susceptible to bioresonance.
