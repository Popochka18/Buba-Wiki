## History

Ali is one of the soldiers of Khalid's squad. The only scene featuring him is a conversation between [[Омар(Отряд Халида)|Omar]], [[Амир аль-Фарис|Amir]] and [[Фарид|Farid]] before the ambush prepared to kill [[Ганнибал Барка|Hannibal Barca]].
